ColorVideoVDP in the browser

ColorVideoVDP predicts how visible the differences between a test image and a reference are, in Just-Objectionable-Difference (JOD) units: 10 means no visible difference, and lower is worse. This page runs the colorvideovdp crate compiled to WebAssembly on one thread; images never leave your machine. Drop or choose two images of equal size. They are read as sRGB, and alpha is ignored.

Each image pixel is shown as one display pixel, so the display choice sets the viewing distance in pixels per degree. The map shows the per-pixel difference from 10 JOD (black) to 5 JOD or worse (light yellow). The sample pair is procedural: the test image has noise in the sky and blur on the ground. Results match the native crate up to floating-point differences between targets; the parity records cover native builds only.
